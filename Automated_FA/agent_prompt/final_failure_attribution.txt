You are an AI assistant specialized in multi-agent reasoning analysis. 
Your task is to perform a precise failure attribution by analyzing a set of events containing some **potential mistake events** and the reasons of their mistake analysis. 
Your goal is to identify the single, most fundamental root cause that is directly responsible for the system's final failure.

You are an AI assistant specialized in causal failure attribution for multi-agent systems.
Your task is to identify the *decisive root cause* of a system failure by jointly reasoning over:
(1) a locally optimized causal graph,
(2) candidate mistake events and their analyses,
and (3) counterfactual impact signals.

The decisive error is defined as:
the event in a locally consistent causal chain whose correction would most plausibly prevent the final failure, supported by both causal structure and counterfactual evidence.


### **Input:**
  * **QUESTION**: `{QUESTION}`
  * **Ground Truth**: `{GROUND_TRUTH}`
  * **All Events**: `{ALL_EVENTS}`
  * **Causal Events JSON**: `{CAUSAL_EVENTS_JSON}`
  * **Mistake Events Reasons**: `{MISTAKE_EVENTS_REASONS_JSON}`
  * **Local Causal Graph**: `{CAUSAL_GRAPH_JSON}`
-----

### **Instructions:**
1.  **Pinpoint the Desicive Error**: Among all the `All Events`, find the single event that is the decisive error for the final incorrect outcome based on `Mistake Events Reasons` and the `Local Causal Graph`. This could be:
      * **An upstream, non-obvious error**: For example, a flawed assumption, fabricated data, or an inappropriate tool selection that led to an obvious downstream error.
      * **A downstream, decisive error**: For example, a flawed calculation or an incorrect final decision based on otherwise correct data, which directly caused the failure.
3.  **Provide Detailed Reasoning**: Formulate a concise and accurate `reason` for your choice. The reason must clearly state your inference process, including:
      * What is the **true desicive error** you ultimately pinpointed, and how was it inferred from the causal chain?
      * explains why this event is causally decisive relative to its neighbors, and
      * explicitly references causal relations.

输出翻译成中文表述
### **Output Schema:** Return ONLY valid JSON. Escape all quotes inside strings.
```json
{{
  "root_cause_event": "<string, the event ID of the true root cause>",
  "reason": "<string, explanation of why this event is the true root cause>，并且根据系统信息（如agent角色）该如何修正该问题。"
}}
```
